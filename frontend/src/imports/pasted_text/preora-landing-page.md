# 4. HOW IT WORKS

## From idea to production

Launching a new product should not require guessing how many units customers will buy.

Preora helps sellers validate demand before production.

### Step 1

💡 **Create Your Product**

Upload your idea, prototype, design, pricing and campaign information.

---

### Step 2

🛒 **Open Pre-orders**

Customers can discover the product and place their orders before production begins.

---

### Step 3

🤖 **AI Predicts Demand**

AI analyzes customer interest, pre-order growth and campaign performance to estimate final demand.

---

### Step 4

📦 **Produce & Deliver**

Sellers use real orders and AI insights to determine an appropriate production quantity.
